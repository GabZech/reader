Why the Fed changed course

The Atlantic · Yesterday · Started · 14 min

Open original

For months the committee had talked as if another increase was the base case. Markets priced that in, and most of the commentary followed.

Inflation prints then came in softer than expected, twice. The tone in the minutes shifted before the public remarks did.

The committee’s language shifted from “further tightening” to “waiting for more evidence,” which is the line that changed the market’s week.
