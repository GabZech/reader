## Morning News Pass (2026-08-19)

Home (and News) items open the article itself, not a per-source digest. In-app reading is always the clean article (text and images; ads and unrelated chrome removed). Top: Close, then Read later and Open original (default browser; dummy states that instead of leaving). After Read later, Close is the same control as on the article (returns to Home or News). Bottom: Mark as seen, same return as Close, does not open the list. List views (News, Favourite channels) have Unseen and Seen at the top, with counts, e.g. Unseen (6) and Seen (2).

## Home contents (2026-08-19)

In the actual app, Home only shows unseen items, and only unarchived Read later items. Mark as seen or archive takes that item off Home. The dummy does not live-update after those taps.

## Read-Later Pass (2026-08-19)

Library / Archive on the list, with counts, not Unseen / Seen. Items show Started or Unstarted. Open is the same clean article: Close, Open original, Archive (same return as Close). A started item resumes at the marked paragraph. Earlier text stays in the article; nothing is summarised or removed. Archive items are scenery; re-access is from the vault, not built here.

## Highlight and Land in Obsidian (2026-08-19)

First-open highlight on the morning article: select text to highlight; tap a highlight to add a section title or a subsection title (this highlight and those that follow, until the next title) or delete it. Title screens show the highlight, an empty field, and Done. Copy text and delete highlight is the recovery when the selection was meant for the clipboard. The dummy does not show the note arriving in the vault.

## Evening Video Triage (2026-08-19)

Favourite channels is on Home. A YouTube item is treated like any other list item: Close, Open original (default browser; dummy states that), Mark as seen (same return as Close). Watching inside the app, the evening session, and filtering Shorts are not in this dummy. Signed off.

## Item row images (2026-08-19)

When an item has a main image (article opening image or video thumbnail), the Home and list row shows a small square on the left. Items without one, including many newsletters, stay text-only. Signed off.

## Home lists (2026-08-19)

Home has Edit at the top right. Home lists: On Home or Hidden for each list, Move up / Move down for order. Done returns to Home with those choices applied. Signed off.

## List edit (2026-08-19)

A list view has Edit at the top right. That screen has the name field, Done, and Delete list. Signed off.

## Source screen (2026-08-19)

Tapping a source opens that source first: See items of this source, Add source to list, Delete source. Add source to list still chooses a list (and a time window if News). After those options, the top-left control is Back. What see-items and delete-source do in the product can wait. Signed off.
